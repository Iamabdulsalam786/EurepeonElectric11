FAQ’S

Frequently Asked
Question

Answers to common questions about quotes, scheduling, permits, and the electrical services we provide for homes and businesses.

  • Do you handle residential electrical work?

    Yes. We install, rewire, and upgrade residential electrical systems — including outlets, fixtures, panels, lighting, safety improvements, and home EV chargers. Every job is scoped with a clear written quote before work begins.

  • Are you licensed and insured?

    European Electric LLC is fully licensed and insured. Our electricians follow current electrical codes and industry safety standards on every residential and commercial project.

  • Do you work on commercial properties?

    Yes. We support tenant improvements, commercial power systems, and commercial lighting for offices, retail, and other business spaces — from small fit-outs to larger electrical build-outs.

  • Can you install a home EV charger?

    We install Level 2 residential EV chargers and upgrade panels or service capacity when needed. We assess your existing electrical setup and recommend the right circuit, location, and permit path.

  • How do I get a quote?

    Call us, use the contact form, or submit a quote request through our appointment page. Describe your project and we will respond within one business day with next steps and a transparent estimate.

  • Do you handle permits and inspections?

    For work that requires permits — panel upgrades, new construction, major rewiring, and many commercial projects — we manage the permit process and ensure the installation is inspection-ready.
