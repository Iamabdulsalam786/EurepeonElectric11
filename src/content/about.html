You Have the Problem. We Have the Electrical Solution.

European Electric LLC is a full-service electrical contractor serving homeowners, property managers, and commercial clients across Melbourne. When something electrical is unsafe, outdated, underpowered, or holding your project back, you have the problem — we have the solution.
